# Encoder comparison: Legal-BERT vs BGE turn vectors

Same recipe as v1 (train 1980+, 6 epochs); network rows are the mean-probability ensemble of seeds 0-2; cutoff 0.5. 'AUC vs v1': 95% CI of the AUC difference to v1, paired bootstrap over cases. case-level = AUC of a case's mean probability against the winner; within-case = AUC of ranking the justices inside non-unanimous cases. Legal-BERT: nlpaueb/legal-bert-base-uncased, mean-pooled last layer. BGE: BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5, [CLS] vector, unit length. Both frozen, 768-d, same chunking of long turns. Test untouched.
